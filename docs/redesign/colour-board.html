Rules that keep it from looking generic

Cyan is light, not paint.

Only where the compass emits it: needle tip, selected meridian, focus, progress. ≤5% of any frame. Never a background, gradient or button fill (one optional signal CTA in Scene 8).

Warm against cold.

Champagne brass and ivory are the warm counterweight to aubergine and cyan — the tension that separates this from purple-cyan AI templates.

Two times of day.

Night scenes alternate with ivory paper scenes; no two consecutive scenes share a ground. No purple→cyan gradients, no glow blobs, no glass.
